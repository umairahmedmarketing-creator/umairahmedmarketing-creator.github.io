01 / About

A quick introduction

I started college in computer engineering because I loved technology. It didn't take long to realize that while I enjoyed understanding how things worked, I didn't want to spend my career designing circuits.

So I made what felt like a big decision at the time: I dropped out of engineering, switched into business marketing, and never looked back.

Studying in Kitchener meant I was surrounded by startups, tech companies, and people building interesting products. Somewhere between lectures and co-op conversations, I discovered product marketing. It was the perfect intersection of technology, business, and storytelling.

Over the past 7+ years, I've worked across enterprise software and cybersecurity, helping technical companies explain complex products in ways that make sense to customers. I've developed positioning and messaging, launched products, built competitive strategies, and enabled sales teams. My favorite part of the job is translating between the people building the product and the people deciding whether to buy it.

That experience has taken me from a telecommunications and government technology company to a product-led SaaS business, and most recently, one of the world's largest cybersecurity vendors. Different industries, different customers, and different go-to-market motions, but the same challenge: helping great products find the right story.

I'm based in Waterloo, open to relocating for the right opportunity, and excited by companies building technical products that solve meaningful problems.

Acronis Compliance Navigator

Acronis · Positioning, customer insight & GTM

The customer signal

A pattern kept coming up in conversations with customers, prospects, partners, and the field: people understood what individual Acronis products did, but struggled to connect those capabilities to the compliance frameworks they were responsible for. The question was usually some version of: "how does Acronis actually help me with the requirements I need to meet?"

The insight

We had plenty of product capabilities and plenty of compliance content. What was missing was the layer connecting the two. Rather than asking customers to start with an Acronis product, I wanted to start where they were already starting: their industry, geography, and compliance obligations.

The approach

I led the positioning and development of the Acronis Compliance Navigator, an interactive experience that lets users filter by industry, country, and compliance framework, then connect individual requirements to CIS Controls and relevant Acronis capabilities. I worked across Product, Solutions Engineering, Compliance, Web, Design, Growth, and Content to bring it together. I also worked with the Center for Internet Security on the control-mapping approach and partnered with internal technical teams to validate how Acronis capabilities mapped back to those controls.

Taking it to market

Building the tool was only part of the job. I developed the messaging around it, built the sales enablement, introduced it through webinars and partner conversations, and worked with regional teams as new framework requests came in. That feedback also helped shape what we added next.

The result

The Navigator grew to cover major frameworks across North America, Europe, the UK, India, Singapore, and Australia. Early traffic showed that roughly two-thirds of visitors were new to Acronis, with engagement around twice the broader site average. More importantly, it gave Sales and partners a much simpler way to answer a question they were already hearing from customers.
