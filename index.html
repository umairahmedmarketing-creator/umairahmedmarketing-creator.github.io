01 / About

A quick introduction

I started university in computer engineering because I loved technology. It didn't take long to realize that while I enjoyed understanding how things worked, I didn't want to spend my career designing circuits.

So I made what felt like a big decision at the time: I dropped out of engineering, switched into business marketing, and never looked back.

Studying in Kitchener meant I was surrounded by startups, tech companies, and people building interesting products. Somewhere between lectures and co-op conversations, I discovered product marketing. It was the perfect intersection of technology, business, and storytelling.

Over the past 7+ years, I've worked across enterprise software and cybersecurity, helping technical companies explain complex products in ways that make sense to customers. I've developed positioning and messaging, launched products, built competitive strategies, and enabled sales teams. My favorite part of the job is translating between the people building the product and the people deciding whether to buy it.

I've worked across a telecommunications and government technology company, a product-led SaaS business, and most recently one of the world's largest cybersecurity vendors. Different industries, different customers, and different go-to-market motions, but the same challenge: helping great products find the right story.

I'm based in Waterloo, open to relocating for the right opportunity, and excited by companies building technical products that solve meaningful problems.

A closer look

A few projects worth walking through in more detail.

Acronis Compliance Navigator

Acronis · Led company-wide compliance positioning

Customers and prospects across regulated industries, healthcare, energy, manufacturing, financial services, government, kept asking the same question: how does Acronis actually help with our compliance obligations? I was tasked with building the company's compliance positioning from the ground up, and led a cross-functional team, developers, a growth marketer, designers, and content marketing, to build the Compliance Navigator: an interactive hub where prospects filter by industry, country, and framework to see requirements mapped to CIS Controls and Acronis products. I also worked directly with CIS (Center for Internet Security) to streamline how each framework maps to their controls, and partnered with Acronis Solutions Engineering and Product to validate how those controls map to real Acronis capabilities. I also built and ran the sales enablement behind the tool.

Live tool: Covers 11 major frameworks, including GDPR, NIS2, DORA, HIPAA, NIST 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001, Cyber Essentials, CAF, SMB 1001, DPDP Act, and Essential 8.
